Audify Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20

Who we are

Audify is a music streaming app for Android. It is published by the Audify developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at france.gelasque.dev@gmail.com.

Summary

Audify has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal information. The app has no ads, analytics or crash reporting, and it does not sell or share personal data.

What the app does with data

DataWhere it goesWhy
Song catalog (titles, artists, cover art, audio links)Downloaded from our Google Firebase Realtime DatabaseTo show the songs you can play
Audio and cover images you playStreamed from our servers and cached on your deviceFaster playback and less data use
Songs you save for offline listeningStored only on your deviceListening without a connection
Technical connection data (IP address, device and network information sent with any internet request)Received by Google Firebase and the servers that host the audio and imagesNeeded to deliver content. Handled under Firebase's privacy terms.

We don't collect what you listen to, search for or save. Your offline songs, cache and settings stay on your phone and are never uploaded.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load the catalog and stream music.
  • Media controls: so headset and media buttons can play, pause and skip.

Audify does not access your microphone, camera, location, contacts or files.

Deleting your data

Because there are no accounts, we hold no personal data about you. To remove everything the app has stored on your phone, delete offline songs in the app, clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

Audify is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Security

Content is delivered over encrypted HTTPS connections where the hosting server supports it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date.
