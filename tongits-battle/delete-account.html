Delete your Tongits Battle account

This page is for the Tongits Battle Android app (package com.francegelasque.tongitsbattle), published by the Tongits Battle developer. See also the privacy policy.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Tongits Battle and sign in (if you are not already).
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Account, choose Delete account.
  4. Read the warning and choose Delete account again to confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Deletion takes effect right away when you confirm. If the app cannot reach the internet, nothing is deleted and you stay signed in, so you can try again.

Delete by email

If you cannot use the app, email france.gelasque.dev@gmail.com from the address linked to your sign-in. Tell us the app name (Tongits Battle) and ask us to delete your account. We will confirm by reply and aim to finish within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your ranked data on our server: points, matches played, and match awards, including the hashed three-player record.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account and its link to your Google, Facebook or Apple sign-in. This includes the name, email, user ID and profile photo URL we received.

What is kept

  • A minimal deletion record on our server, holding only the time of deletion under your user ID. It stops sign-in tokens issued before the deletion from recreating your ranked data.
  • Server logs held by Cloudflare, under its own retention rules.
  • Data on your phone: chips, wallet, card backs and settings are not uploaded and are not removed by account deletion. Uninstalling the app removes them.

If you never signed in

Guest play keeps data only on your device. There is nothing on our server to delete. Uninstall the app to remove it.

Your Google, Facebook or Apple account

Deleting your Tongits Battle account does not delete your Google, Facebook or Apple account. You can remove the app's access in that provider's account settings.
