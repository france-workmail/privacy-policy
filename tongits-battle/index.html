Tongits Battle Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-20

This policy covers the Tongits Battle Android app (package com.francegelasque.tongitsbattle). To delete your account and data, see how to delete your account.

Who we are

Tongits Battle is a Tongits card game. It is published by the Tongits Battle developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at france.gelasque.dev@gmail.com.

Data we collect

You can play offline against bots without an account. Signing in is optional.

Data we collect, and why

Stored only on your device and never uploaded: your chips and wallet, card backs bought with in-game chips, and your settings. There are no real-money purchases in the app, and chips have no cash value.

What we do not collect: we do not use an analytics SDK or crash reporting, and the app does not use location, contacts, camera or microphone. The app asks for these Android permissions: internet, network state, Wi-Fi state, and Wi-Fi multicast (used to find LAN games).

How we use data

  • To sign you in and keep your account.
  • To run ranked matches, keep your points and prevent farming.
  • To show your display name to the players in your room.
  • To show ads through Google AdMob.
  • To keep the service working and fix errors.

Third parties

These services handle data for us or through the app. Each has its own privacy policy.

  • Google / Firebase Authentication (sign-in): Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (ads, with consent through Google's User Messaging Platform): Google Privacy Policy
  • Facebook (optional sign-in). Facebook's automatic event logging and advertiser-ID collection are turned off in the app: Meta Privacy Policy
  • Apple (optional sign-in): Apple Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare (hosts our ranked and online-room server): Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Ads are not directed at children.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the services listed above, as needed to run the app, and with the other players in your room as described (your display name). We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Retention

  • Account and ranked data are kept until you delete your account.
  • The hashed three-player record is pruned after 24 hours.
  • Server logs are kept by Cloudflare under its own retention rules.
  • Data on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Deletion

In the app, open Settings, find Account, and choose Delete account. This deletes your ranked data on our server and your Firebase account. You can also email us at france.gelasque.dev@gmail.com. Full details are on the account deletion page. If you never signed in, your data is only on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it.

Security

Connections to our server use HTTPS and secure WebSockets (WSS). LAN play talks directly between phones over your local network. No method is perfectly secure, but we limit what we store to what the features need.

Children

Tongits Battle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version. We will publish changes on this page.
